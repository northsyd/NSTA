About Us

The Transport Association is an independent, non‑profit organisation dedicated to advancing safe, sustainable, and efficient mobility across Australia.

We bring together researchers, policymakers, industry leaders, and practitioners to develop evidence‑based solutions for the future of transport.

Our Mission

To support a transport system that is safe, resilient, low‑emissions, and accessible to all Australians.

What We Do

  • Conduct independent research and analysis
  • Publish reports, briefs, and expert commentary
  • Host events, workshops, and policy dialogues
  • Support innovation in mobility and infrastructure
