About

A student-led association for serious local transport advocacy.

NSTA exists to research, discuss and advocate for better transport outcomes in North Sydney and across Sydney.

What we are

Local, practical and future-facing.

The North Sydney Transport Association is an independent student-led transport advocacy association. It produces submissions, proposals, explainers and public-facing transport commentary.

NSTA focuses on public transport, corridor planning, walking, cycling, transport history and long-term infrastructure opportunities.

NSTA is not affiliated with Transport for NSW, North Sydney Council, or any school unless clearly stated.
