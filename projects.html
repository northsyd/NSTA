Projects

Ongoing campaigns and transport ideas.

Each project should explain the problem, the proposal, why it matters, and what NSTA is doing about it.

Corridor planning

Military Road future mass transit

Advocating for bus corridor works to preserve the possibility of light rail or another high-capacity transit mode.

  • Problem: corridor capacity is limited.
  • Proposal: design upgrades to be convertible.
  • Status: council advocacy and submission work.

Regional access

Northern Beaches rail or light rail advocacy

Developing long-term options for better connections between the Northern Beaches, Lower North Shore and Sydney CBD.

  • Problem: heavy reliance on buses.
  • Proposal: protect and study mass transit corridors.
  • Status: concept development.

Rail infrastructure

North Sydney tunnel stubs

Researching the unused tunnel stubs and how they could relate to future rail-based transport options.

  • Problem: historic provisions remain unused.
  • Proposal: keep future usage on the agenda.
  • Status: briefing material.

Active transport

Rail corridor cycleway

Exploring whether a high-quality cycle route could fit within parts of the North Shore rail corridor.

  • Problem: limited protected cycling routes.
  • Proposal: investigate corridor opportunities.
  • Status: MP/campaign suggestion.
